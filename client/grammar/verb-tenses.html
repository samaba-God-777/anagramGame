📚 English Verb Tenses

12 tenses organized by time (Present, Past, Future) and aspect (Simple, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous).

🕐 Present Tenses

Present Simple A1

Habits, routines, general truths, permanent states.

Subject + verb(s/es)
Present Continuous A2

Actions happening now, temporary situations, future plans.

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing
Present Perfect B1

Past actions with present results, experiences, unfinished time.

Subject + have/has + past participle
Present Perfect Continuous B1

Actions that started in the past and continue to now, duration emphasis.

Subject + have/has + been + verb-ing

🕑 Past Tenses

Past Simple A1

Completed actions at a specific time in the past.

Subject + verb-ed / irregular past
Past Continuous A2

Ongoing past actions, background events, interrupted actions.

Subject + was/were + verb-ing
Past Perfect B1

Actions completed before another past action.

Subject + had + past participle
Past Perfect Continuous C1

Ongoing actions before another past event, duration emphasis.

Subject + had + been + verb-ing

🕒 Future Tenses

Future Simple A1

Predictions, spontaneous decisions, promises, future facts.

Subject + will + verb
Future Continuous A2

Actions in progress at a future time, polite inquiries.

Subject + will + be + verb-ing
Future Perfect B2

Actions completed before a specific future time.

Subject + will + have + past participle
Future Perfect Continuous B2

Duration of an action up to a point in the future.

Subject + will + have + been + verb-ing
